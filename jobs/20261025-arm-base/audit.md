## Notes (do not block)

1. **No `M907 E430`.** The flattened stock profiles have no `print_settings_id`, so the stock `{if print_settings_id=~/.*QUALITY @MK3.*/}M907 E430` line is skipped and the extruder motor runs at the firmware's default current. Nothing was added; not a safety issue. Fix for later: keep the preset names in the profile files.
2. **Window seam lines.** Two vertical seam lines on the window's +X end face (the near-tab end), over the plate and pad. A seam bump eats into the 0.2 mm end clearance. If the MG996R is tight going in, scrape those two lines with a knife.
3. **Bed sheet.** Smooth PEI only (`config/printer.json`): PETG needs a glue-stick release layer (preflight). The 4 mm solid plate on the bed adds shrink stress: let the sheet cool before removing the part.
4. **Outside this gate.** `strength.md` is NEEDS_TEST: spec §8's proof test applies after assembly.
